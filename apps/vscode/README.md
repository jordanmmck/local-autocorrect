# Local Autocorrect

Fixes typos as you type, the way a phone keyboard does. It runs entirely on your machine: no network, no telemetry, no popups.

Type `teh ` and get `the `. Undo once to keep what you typed, and that correction won't be made again this session.

Active in Markdown and plain text by default. Click **Autocorrect** in the status bar to turn it off.

See the [project README](https://github.com/jordanmmck/local-autocorrect#readme) for settings, how it works and known limitations.
